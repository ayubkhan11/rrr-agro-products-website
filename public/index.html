Our Founder

R. Ramesh

Founder & Chief Executive

With over a decade of deep-rooted experience in the coconut industry, R. Ramesh founded RRR Agro Products in 2022 with a singular vision — to position Indian coconuts at the forefront of global export markets. His journey from the lush plantations of Pollachi to the international trade arena reflects an unwavering commitment to quality, authenticity, and sustainable agricultural practices.

Our Story

Built for The World

Nestled in the coconut heartland of Pollachi, Tamil Nadu, RRR Agro Products sources the finest coconuts from carefully selected plantations. Every product undergoes stringent quality checks to meet international export standards — from farm to global destination.
